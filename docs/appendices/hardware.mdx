# Appendix A: Hardware Requirements

This appendix outlines the recommended hardware specifications for effectively engaging with the practical examples and simulations presented in this book. While some theoretical concepts can be understood with minimal hardware, a capable development machine is essential for hands-on experience, especially for NVIDIA Isaac Sim and AI model training.

## 1. Primary Development Workstation (Recommended)

A high-performance workstation is recommended for the best experience, particularly for Module 3 (NVIDIA Isaac) and Module 4 (VLA).

*   **Operating System**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (64-bit)
    *   *Rationale*: ROS 2 Humble/Iron is primarily developed and tested on Ubuntu.
*   **Processor**: Intel Core i7 (10th Gen or newer) or AMD Ryzen 7 (3000 series or newer) or equivalent.
*   **RAM**: 32 GB or more.
    *   *Rationale*: Large simulations and AI model training can consume significant memory.
*   **GPU**: NVIDIA RTX 30 Series (e.g., RTX 3080, 3090) or RTX 40 Series (e.g., RTX 4080, 4090) with at least 10 GB VRAM (12 GB+ recommended).
    *   *Rationale*: Essential for NVIDIA Isaac Sim, Isaac ROS, CUDA acceleration, and efficient AI model inference/training. Ensure up-to-date NVIDIA drivers (version 525.60.11 or later).
*   **Storage**: 1 TB NVMe SSD or larger.
    *   *Rationale*: Fast I/O is crucial for loading large simulation assets and datasets.

## 2. Edge Deployment Hardware (Optional, for advanced topics)

For exploring real-world deployment and embedded AI concepts, an NVIDIA Jetson development kit is highly recommended.

*   **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Developer Kit** (16 GB RAM) or **Jetson Orin NX Developer Kit**.
    *   *Rationale*: These provide a powerful, energy-efficient platform for running Isaac ROS GEMs and deploying trained AI models to physical robots.
*   **Accessories**: Power supply, microSD card (for Orin Nano), camera (e.g., Raspberry Pi Camera Module V2 or compatible CSI camera), networking.

## 3. Sensors (Optional, for hands-on physical robot interaction)

*   **Intel RealSense Depth Camera (D435i or D455)**:
    *   *Rationale*: Widely used for RGB-D perception, VSLAM, and object detection. ROS 2 drivers are readily available.
*   **IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit)**: Often integrated into development boards or robot platforms.
*   **Microphone**: For voice command input in VLA systems.

## 4. Cloud Computing (Alternative/Supplemental)

While not a primary focus for hands-on exercises in this book due to cost and latency for interactive learning, cloud platforms can offer scalable alternatives:

*   **NVIDIA Omniverse Cloud**: Provides access to Isaac Sim instances in the cloud, useful for large-scale simulations or if a local RTX workstation is unavailable.
*   **AWS EC2 Instances (e.g., g5/g6e instances)**: GPU-accelerated virtual machines can be used for deep learning training and running compute-intensive simulations.
